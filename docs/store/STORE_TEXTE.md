## English
**App name:** Fesara – Run & Surf
**Short description (max. 80):** Run, jump and surf through the neon city – collect stars and boards!

**Full description:**
Run and surf through the city! In Fesara your hero dashes through colourful streets – until the street turns into a canal: jump on your board, ride the waves and pull off mid-air flips for bonus points!

• Levels with a finish line and up to 3 stars
• Surf sections with waves, rocks and piers
• Mid-air flips for bonus points
• Power-ups: hoverboard, magnet, shield and double points
• 3 new daily missions with rewards
• Board shop – game coins only, no real money
• Outfit by text: "pink hair, black hoodie" – and your hero wears it
• Simple controls: swipe or arrow keys
• English and German

Made for kids: no ads, no real-money purchases, no data collection, plays offline.

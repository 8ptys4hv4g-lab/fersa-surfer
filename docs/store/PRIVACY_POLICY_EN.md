# Privacy Policy – Fersa Wave

**Last updated:** 15 November 2026

**Fersa Wave does not collect, store or share any personal data.**

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No internet connection: the game sends no data to us or to third parties.
- No advertising and no advertising IDs.
- No analytics or tracking services.
- No real-money purchases (in-game coins only).
- No access to camera, microphone, location or contacts.

**Stored on your device only:** score/best score, in-game coins, unlocked boards, level stars, mission progress and settings (volume, graphics, language). This data never leaves the device and is deleted when the app is uninstalled.

**Children:** As no data is collected, no data from children is collected.

**Changes:** If this ever changes (e.g. an online feature), we will update this policy beforehand and obtain parental consent where required.
